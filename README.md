# Bright-Coffee-Shop-Case-Study
Bright Coffee Shop Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This project analyzes transactional sales data from Bright Coffee Shop to provide business insights that support decision-making for the newly appointed CEO. The analysis focuses on identifying revenue-generating products, understanding peak sales periods, evaluating store performance, and recommending strategies to drive business growth.

The project follows a complete data analytics workflow, including data preparation, transformation, SQL analysis, dashboard development, and executive reporting.

Business Problem

Bright Coffee Shop's CEO aims to improve revenue growth and product performance across all store locations.

The business requires answers to the following questions:
Which products generate the most revenue?
What time of day performs best?
Which store locations contribute the most sales?
What sales trends exist across products and time periods?
What actions can be implemented to increase revenue and improve performance?

Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project were:
Identify top revenue-generating products
Analyze sales performance by category and location
Determine peak sales periods
Evaluate store performance
Develop business recommendations based on data-driven insights
Create visual dashboards and executive reports

Methodology
1. Project Planning
Created a Brainstorm Diagram
Developed a Project Timeline (Gantt Chart)

2. Data Processing
Converted raw data into CSV format
Uploaded the dataset into Databricks
Cleaned and transformed the data
Corrected data types and formatting issues
Created calculated fields

3. Data Transformation
The following calculations were performed:

Total Revenue = Unit Price × Transaction Quantity

Additional transformations included:
Revenue calculation
Time bucket creation
Product categorization
Store-level aggregation

4. SQL Analysis
SQL queries were used to analyze:
Revenue by product category
Revenue by product
Revenue by store location
Revenue by time intervals
Best-selling products
Product performance trends

5. Data Visualization
Pivot Tables and dashboards were created to visualize:
Revenue performance
Product performance
Store performance
Peak sales periods
Sales trends

6. Executive Reporting
Findings and recommendations were presented in a PowerPoint presentation designed for executive decision-making.

Key Insights
Coffee products generated the highest revenue contribution.
Morning trading periods recorded the strongest sales performance.
Store performance was relatively balanced across locations.
Bakery products showed strong cross-selling opportunities with beverages.
Premium products generated higher revenue per transaction despite lower sales volumes.

Recommendations
Focus marketing efforts during peak sales periods.
Promote top-performing products and categories.
Introduce product bundles and upselling opportunities.
Increase promotion of underperforming product categories.
Implement automated sales reporting and performance monitoring.
